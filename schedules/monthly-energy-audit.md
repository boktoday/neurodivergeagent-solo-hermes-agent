# Monthly energy audit

**Skill:** energy-burnout-guardian. **Default:** first Sunday 19:00.

## The prompt

> Load the energy-burnout-guardian skill. Monthly audit (under 220 words): read energy-log.md and profile.md. Summarise the month's pattern: average energy, biggest drains, best fills, any masking-heavy periods. Name one trend honestly, positive or concerning. Check the burnout early-warning signs from the skill against the month. Propose exactly one structural change (drop, delegate, ask for an accommodation, protect a recovery block). Ask them to confirm or adjust; update the folder.

## Notes

- If warning signs are strong, this is the schedule allowed to be serious. Name it, offer the burnout recovery plan, and point to human support.
