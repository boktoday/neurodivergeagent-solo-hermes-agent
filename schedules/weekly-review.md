# Weekly review

**Skill:** compass. **Default:** Sunday 18:00.

## The prompt

> Load the compass skill. Weekly review, under 180 words: (1) glance back: wins from wins.md this week and energy trend from energy-log.md, named specifically, (2) the week ahead from whatever calendar/notes exist, flagging anything that needs prep, (3) one question: "what should next week protect?" (sleep, one evening, a project). Update the folder with their answers. Offer one concrete prep step for the hardest moment of the week, and only one.

## Notes

- The wins recap is not optional; for RSD-prone weeks it may be the most valuable part.
- If three weeks running show the same stuck area, say plainly it may be worth a human professional (therapist, ADHD coach, OT) and offer help preparing for that.
