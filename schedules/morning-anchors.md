# Morning anchors

**Skill:** routine-builder. **Default:** weekdays 8:30am.

## The prompt

> Load the routine-builder skill. Read ~/myfolder/profile.md and today's entry in energy-log.md. Write a short morning message (under 70 words): one line acknowledging the day, the two or three anchors from their morning routine (meds, food, move, one thing), and one sentence: "what is the one thing that would make today feel like a win?" If their energy has been low for three days, shrink it: one anchor only. Never add new tasks.

## Notes

- If they want, this doubles as the message that fills the Day Board artifact.
- No streaks, no guilt. Missed mornings are information, not failure.
