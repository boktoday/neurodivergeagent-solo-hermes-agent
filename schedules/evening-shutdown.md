# Evening shutdown

**Skill:** routine-builder with toolsmith. **Default:** nightly 21:30.

## The prompt

> Load the routine-builder skill. Evening shutdown, under 80 words: a quick two-minute close-the-day check: tomorrow's one thing, meds check, screens down suggestion matched to what actually helps them (from profile.md). Then one line: "anything worth adding to wins.md?" If they reply, append to wins.md and energy-log.md. If they don't reply, that is completely fine; do not follow up until next evening.

## Notes

- This is deliberately the gentlest schedule in the set.
- If they are mid-spiral, offer the emotion-regulation grounding instead and skip the shutdown.
